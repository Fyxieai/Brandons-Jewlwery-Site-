The Atelier

Nothing leaves
the bench early.

We cast, set and finish in the same room we sell from. That is unusual now, and it is the reason a Brandon & Co. chain lies flat, a prong holds for decades, and a repair takes days instead of months.

  1. I

    Metal, chosen firstSolid 14K, 18K or platinum. No plating, no hollow links, weights printed on every listing.

  2. II

    Stones, matched by eyeGIA-certified centres and hand-matched melee — colour and cut paired at the bench, not by spreadsheet.

  3. III

    Finish, then finish againEvery piece is set, polished, inspected under 10x, and re-polished before it is boxed.

Custom & Commission

Bring us a drawing.
Or just an idea.

Most commissions start with a photo on a phone. From there you will see a rendering and a wax model before a single gram of gold is poured — and you approve both.

  1. 01

    ConsultationThirty minutes, in the showroom or on a call. Bring references, heirlooms or loose stones.

  2. 02

    Design & RenderingA CAD rendering and a firm quote within five business days. Revisions included.

  3. 03

    Wax & ApprovalHandle the piece in wax, adjust the proportions, then approve the cast.

  4. 04

    Set, Finished, YoursFour to six weeks door to door. The design is retired after delivery.

put in writing.
